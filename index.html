About Spring Inspiration

Spring Inspiration is a student-driven fashion committee at Jönköping University, creating an annual runway show that celebrates creativity and sustainable design. The show features student collections from upcycled garments, alongside designs by international designers — bringing together innovation, craftsmanship and global perspective.
